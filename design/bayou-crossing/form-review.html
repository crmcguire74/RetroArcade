Planned integration places this cabinet in the right-hand VECTOR bay. The arcade selector and guided navigation will offer Play Bayou Crossing. The game begins with a visible Start crossing button and simple directional controls.

Traffic moves through five lanes. Logs drift and carry you. Turtles warn before they dive. Five homes complete a round; three rounds complete a run. High scores are stored separately from Brickstorm.

The gameplay simulation has passed a complete three-round route check: 15 homes, 289 hops, all 3 lives retained. This proves a playable path through the rules; browser controls and headset comfort still need runtime verification.
